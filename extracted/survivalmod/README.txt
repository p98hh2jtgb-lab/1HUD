HUD-I NE EKRAN: IDENTIK me HUD PRO v5.1 — te njejtat shkronja (Futura Extra Bold),
emoji, animacione, ngjyra (jeshile > portokalli > kuqe). Asgje e ndryshuar.

PANELI I OPSIONEVE: look i re (graphite + i bardhe, nje akcent, zero perplasje ngjyrash).
5 tabs: PAMJA / NGJYRAT / EFEKTET / FORMULA / TJERA — te gjitha opsionet e v5.1:
pozicion + drag, madhesia, layout (inline/stacked/compact), background (none/pill/card),
statusi + emoji, mini meter, -X% delta, rolling counter, impact shake, low pulse,
visibility modes, formulat, pesha, presets, tastet.

TASTET: V = ON/OFF · R = reset run · H = paneli (te ndryshueshme ne TJERA).
Lidhjet e forta: Options -> Controls -> "SURVIVAL HUD: ..."

INSTALIMI: vendose ZIP ne Documents/BeamNG.drive/mods (mos e ekstrakto), restart BeamNG.
